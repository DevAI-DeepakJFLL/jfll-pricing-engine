# Comprehensive Overview: Air Export General Cargo & Billing Workflow

This document provides a unified summary of the air export general cargo process, rate management, and billing workflow based on your conversation with your team lead.

---

## 1. Pre-Shipment, Rates & Airline Surcharges

* **Rate Validity & Negotiations:** Rate validity can span across specific dates (e.g., starting from the 28th). Rate negotiations and matching with airline rates continue dynamically right up until the shipment actually departs.
* **Airline Rate Matching:** The pricing team coordinates closely to match and confirm rates directly with the airline before dispatch.
* **Managing Fuel Surcharges:** Carriers like Emirates update fuel surcharges almost weekly. If an airline increases its fuel surcharge (e.g., buy rate increases by 10), the selling price must be adjusted upward by the exact same amount (e.g., selling price increases by 10) to ensure your profit margin remains protected.

## 2. Booking and System Entry

* **Initial Booking:** The process starts when a customer confirms a booking. 
* **Software Integration:** The booking team moves the record from RFQ to booking inside the shipment software and generates a system quotation using the pricing team's approved rates (e.g., buy rate of 100 and sell rate of 110).

## 3. Execution & Handover

* **Operations:** The pricing team manages shipment execution, tracking flight schedules, handover times, and customs clearance statuses. Pure forwarding shipments proceed directly to handover.
* **Billing Request:** Using the execution date as a reference, the pricing team initiates the billing request by emailing the billing team via the customer communication thread, citing the agreed buy and sell rates.

## 4. Verification, Discrepancies & Feedback

* **Cross-Tallying:** The billing team cross-verifies the system quotation against the pricing team's email request and airline rates.
* **Correction Loops:** If the billing team identifies any mistakes, discrepancies, or requested changes, they reply with feedback outlining the necessary corrections before the invoice is finalized.

## 5. Invoicing, Drafts, and Document Delivery

* **Invoicing Format:** Invoices are primarily handled digitally via email using soft copies. However, if a client specifically requires a physical hard copy, they are dispatched on a **monthly basis**.
* **Draft Approvals:** For multi-location agent clients who frequently need address adjustments or revisions, draft invoices are shared for approval within **24 hours** to minimize later rework.
* **Timeline Targets:** The core performance target is to successfully raise and dispatch the final invoice within **two days** of shipment execution.
